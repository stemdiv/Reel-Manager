Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need a Google Cloud account or API key?

No. You just sign in with your normal Google account and approve access. All the technical plumbing is handled for you.

Will Reel change my playlists without asking?

No. Reel only makes changes you trigger yourself — and it asks for edit permission separately, the first time you actually modify something.

Is my data safe?

Your login stays between your browser and Google. Reel doesn't sell or share your data, and you can wipe everything with the "Delete my data" button, which also revokes access.

Do I have to pay to watch my videos?

Never. Playback is always free and unrestricted. Premium only adds organization and productivity features.

Which accounts work?

Any YouTube account. Your YouTube Music playlists share the same library, so they work too.
